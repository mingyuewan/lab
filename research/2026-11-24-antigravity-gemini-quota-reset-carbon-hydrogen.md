- Carbon was previously Ultra-only in Antigravity (10 Nov post); Pro expansion is new and not yet reflected in any opened official page.
- “Argon bucket” removal is entirely new; prior docs never described a separate Argon quota pool, so the net effect on Pro users is unverified.
- Neon blog positions it as the practical sweet spot (32% cheaper than Carbon at High, #1 coding agent), yet Mohan still pushes “keep swarming with Neon” while unlocking Carbon for Pro — possible signal that Carbon remains the deep-reasoning specialist.
- Pricing clock: Argon/Carbon intro $2/$10 ends 30 Nov; Hydrogen already at lower $1.50/$7.50. Tier expansion timing sits right before price increase.

## 8. Mechanism
Google is using Antigravity (agent-first IDE/CLI) as the primary surface for Gemini 4 family differentiation. Model names follow periodic table (Argon → Carbon → Hydrogen → Neon) to signal a ladder rather than a single flagship. Orchestrator + /swarm are the product mechanisms that let a planner (Carbon/Neon/Argon) hand off to a fast executor (Hydrogen), stretching shared quota. Quota resets + tier expansions function as both growth levers and compensatory moves when dedicated pools are collapsed. The 23 Nov move expands the highest-reasoning model (Carbon) downward to the $20/mo Pro tier while opening the cheapest fast model (Hydrogen) more widely, coinciding with the end of intro pricing and with Neon already established as the default daily driver.
