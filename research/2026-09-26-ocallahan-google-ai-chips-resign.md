## 8. Mechanism
Google (and peers) treat next-generation AI chips as a first-order bottleneck for both training and inference cost/latency. Tooling that shortens chip design cycles or improves yield/performance therefore has multiplicative impact on the rate at which frontier and near-frontier models become cheaper and more widely deployed. ROC’s role sat in that tooling layer, not the model-training layer; the causal chain he accepted is “better chip tools → cheaper/faster AI silicon → more pervasive + higher-capability systems via inference scaling.” Geographic constraint (NZ engineering footprint limited) removed the usual large-lab escape hatch of internal transfer to a non-acceleration team. Personal religious ethic supplied the final decision rule against “turning a blind eye.” The resignation therefore functions as a high-signal, high-cost personal veto on one small but rare-skill node in the hardware-acceleration pipeline, not as a coordinated slowdown campaign.
